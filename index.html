BEHIND THE WEBSITE

Built with purpose.

The following details explain how the website is structured, supported, promoted and maintained.

Content & navigation

The one-page structure uses clear sections: Home, Shop, Our Story, Brand Purpose, Reviews and Website Information. The header links take visitors directly to each section.

Platform & development

Built with HTML5 for structure, CSS3 for layout and responsive styling, and vanilla JavaScript for the mobile menu, demo cart and form feedback. No framework or database is required.

Domain & hosting plan

A real launch could use a registered domain such as yuk t acosmetics.in (subject to availability and legal checks) and static hosting such as GitHub Pages, Netlify or a suitable commercial host. Domain registration and hosting are not included in this academic demo.

Performance & devices

The page uses lightweight CSS product illustrations instead of large product photographs, a small number of local files and responsive breakpoints for phones, tablets and desktops. Google Fonts load externally; system font fallbacks are provided.

SEO & digital marketing

Includes a descriptive page title, meta description, relevant keywords, Open Graph preview metadata, semantic headings and descriptive text. Suggested keywords: Yukta Cosmetics, lip gloss, peach lip gloss, rose lip gloss and nude lip gloss.

Promotion plan

Share shade swatches, short application videos and launch posts on Instagram and Pinterest; use consistent brand colours; link social posts to the Shop section; and review visitor engagement before planning paid campaigns.

Technical note: This is a front-end prototype. The cart is a browser demonstration only; there is no checkout, payment processing, customer account, order database or live email subscription.

RESPONSIBLE WEBSITE DESIGN

Privacy, safety
& accessibility.

Clear information and accessible controls help visitors use a website with confidence.

01

Privacy

The sample sign-up form displays a confirmation message only. It does not transmit or save an email address. A real business must publish an accurate privacy notice before collecting personal data.

02

Security

A live version should use HTTPS, keep software and hosting settings updated, validate form data on the server and use a trusted payment provider. This demo does not request payment or sensitive information.

03

Accessibility

Semantic page regions, labelled controls, keyboard focus styles, a skip link, responsive text and reduced-motion support are included. Product illustrations have descriptive labels.

Read the demo privacy notice

Yukta Cosmetics is a fictional academic brand concept. This website does not operate a real shop and does not collect, transmit or store personal information. The email field is a local demonstration and its contents are cleared after the sample confirmation. No payment details or customer accounts are requested. A real business website would need a full privacy policy, contact information, data retention rules and appropriate consent mechanisms before collecting visitor information.
